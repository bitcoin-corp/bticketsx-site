    How it will work

    From the box office to the door.

    A ticket that lives in your own wallet, not in an inbox or a screenshot. Here is what we are building.

    1. 1

      Find the event

      On bTicketsX, in bCalX, on the artist's page or from a link in a bChatX room.

      Coming
    2. 2

      One price, up front

      The cost you see is the cost you pay, fees included. No extras added at the last step.

      Coming
    3. 3

      It lands in your wallet

      The ticket goes straight to your bWalletX, on your phone or the web, with the event, your entry and the doors time.

      Coming
    4. 4

      A code that changes

      At the door your ticket shows a code signed by your wallet. It changes every 30 seconds, so copies and screenshots don't get in.

      Coming
    5. 5

      Resell it

      Can't go? Resell your ticket through bTicketsX. Your old ticket is cancelled and the buyer's is issued fresh, so the one at the door is always the real one.

      Coming
    6. 6

      Keep the stub

      After the show your ticket stays in your wallet as a stub: proof you were there, and first word on the next one.

      Coming

    Starting with RSVP tickets at no cost. We'll run our own events on bTicketsX first, so the door code and the scanner are proven before anyone pays for a ticket.

    For organisers

    Your event. Paid as you sell.

    Coming for promoters, clubs, venues, comedy nights and meetups. Any organiser, any size.

    Make an event in minutes

    Sign in with bWalletX. Set the tiers, how many, the sale window and the age limit, then publish.

    Paid as you sell

    Ticket money goes straight to you as each ticket sells, not weeks after the show.

    A scanner on any phone

    Open the door scanner in a browser. It keeps working when the venue signal drops, and catches a ticket used twice.

    Resale you can see

    Every resale goes through bTicketsX, so you see it happen, fakes and copies don't get in, and you can set a resale cap for your event if you want one.

    A room for every event

    Each event gets a bChatX room for ticket holders and crew. Tell everyone doors are late, in one place.

    Splits signed up front

    Agree the split with the venue and the acts in bSignX before the sale starts.
